## Contents Summary
The pack contains 64 authentic modular assets designed for 1930s steam-era railway infrastructure:
- **Locomotives**: Inside-framed 0-6-0 goods locomotive with working valve gear & animated wheel-roll; narrow-gauge saddle tank locomotive.
- **Rolling Stock**: Passenger composite carriages, goods brake vans, open wagons, cattle wagons, box vans, mineral hoppers, ballast hoppers.
- **Track Infrastructure**: Modular straight rails, curved sections, turnouts (points), crossovers, diamond crossings, buffer stops.
- **Station Infrastructure**: Platforms, ramps, waiting shelters, platform canopies, passenger footbridges, station clock, station nameboards.
- **Signaling & Mechanical**: High semaphore home/distant signals, ground disc shunt signals, authentic two-story signal box with levers.
- **Depot & Servicing**: Engine shed, turntable, coaling stage, water crane tower, goods shed with crane.
- **Assembled Reference Scenes**:
  - `starter/starter-country-station.glb` (Country station with passenger train, platform, signal box, footbridge, 30,888 tris)
