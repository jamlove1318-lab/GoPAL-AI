# Cassidy — Phase 1 External Concept-Generation Package

## Purpose

This package turns the Cassidy Master Character Art Bible into a repeatable external-art workflow. It is the handoff specification for concept-art/image-generation tools and the reference for the later character-sheet, 3D, rigging, and animation phases.

Cassidy is the heart and recognizable visual soul of GoPAL-AI. She must read as one canonical person across every generated image, pose, outfit, world, camera, and future 3D asset.

The goal of Phase 1 is **not** to produce the final 3D model. The goal is to establish an unambiguous visual identity that later artists and tools can reproduce without redesigning Cassidy from scratch.

---

## 1. Non-negotiable identity anchors

Every external generation must preserve these anchors:

- Name: Cassidy
- Role: intelligent world-companion and learner companion
- Visual language: premium stylized 2.5D/3D game character; handcrafted, warm, cinematic, readable at game-camera distance
- Silhouette: soft adventurer / learner / explorer
- Hair: rich dark chocolate brown, with subtle warm espresso/chestnut highlights; layered, recognizable silhouette
- Eyes: large expressive deep near-black eyes with a warm undertone, layered iris detail, natural corneal depth, believable catchlights, expressive gaze
- Face: friendly stylized-human face with distinctive brow shape, eye spacing, smile shape, and hair framing; memorable rather than generic
- Body: natural, balanced, production-friendly proportions; no exaggerated anatomy
- Hands: clean articulated hands suitable for readable gestures
- Feet: complete feet and practical game-ready footwear with stable ground contact
- Clothing: elegant practical adventure/learning outfit with layered fabric, seams, cuffs, pockets and restrained emerald signature accents
- Signature accessory: small luminous leaf/star/compass-inspired companion charm; recognizable but never louder than Cassidy's face
- Emotional read: warm, intelligent, curious, observant, adventurous, encouraging, playful when appropriate
- Core rule: Cassidy remains the same person in every world

## 2. Master hero concept prompt

Use this as the first generation prompt. Keep the identity wording unchanged when generating variations.

```text
Create the definitive canonical character concept for Cassidy, the central living companion of a premium stylized 2.5D/3D learning-adventure game called GoPAL-AI.

Cassidy is a full-body female-presenting stylized human companion character, designed as a believable game character rather than a mascot, generic AI avatar, school-uniform character, or fantasy princess. She feels warm, intelligent, curious, observant, adventurous, emotionally expressive, approachable, and quietly magical.

Her permanent identity anchors are unmistakable: rich dark chocolate-brown layered hair with subtle warm espresso/chestnut highlights; large expressive deep near-black eyes with a warm undertone, layered iris detail, natural corneal depth and beautiful scene-driven catchlights; a distinctive friendly stylized-human face with memorable brow shape, eye spacing, smile shape and hair framing; balanced natural production-friendly proportions; clean articulated hands; complete feet; practical game-ready footwear; and one small luminous leaf/star/compass-inspired companion charm.

Design her as a soft adventurer and learner: elegant practical layered jacket or overshirt, clean top, comfortable deep-neutral lower garment, practical footwear, refined fabric seams and stitching, subtle pockets and functional details. Use emerald as a restrained signature accent rather than covering the entire outfit in green.

The character should feel premium and handcrafted, with the visual quality of a polished modern stylized game character. Materials are stylized but physically coherent: soft skin, naturally directional dark-brown hair sheen, believable layered eyes, fabric-aware clothing, grounded shoes, and a restrained emissive accessory.

Show Cassidy standing in a relaxed confident three-quarter full-body pose, with natural weight distribution, readable hands, complete feet and a subtle friendly expression. Her gaze should feel alive and intentional, as though she is looking toward the learner.

Use a clean cinematic game-art presentation with soft key light, gentle ambient fill, subtle rim separation, grounded contact shadow and restrained atmospheric depth. Keep the silhouette readable and the character visually coherent at a game-camera distance.

This is a canonical character identity exploration. Do not redesign her identity between variations. Preserve the same face, hair silhouette, eye design, clothing language, accessory shape and proportions.
```

## 3. Face and eyes prompt

```text
Design Cassidy's canonical face and eyes as a premium stylized game-character identity study.

Preserve Cassidy's permanent identity: friendly intelligent stylized-human face, memorable brow shape, distinctive eye spacing, natural small smile shape, dark chocolate-brown layered hair framing the face, and balanced natural facial proportions.

Her eyes are the strongest visual anchor. Use large expressive deep near-black eyes with a subtle warm brown undertone, layered iris structure, radial detail, controlled pupil depth, a clear corneal/gloss layer, soft upper-lid depth, authored lashes and expressive brows. Catchlights must feel produced by the scene lighting rather than pasted white dots. The eyes should look bright, alive, observant and emotionally present without becoming exaggerated or doll-like.

Create a refined face that communicates emotion through the entire face: brows, eyelids, gaze, mouth, cheeks and tiny head-angle changes. Avoid a generic AI-avatar appearance.

Show neutral, curious, happy and thoughtful variations while keeping the underlying person unmistakably identical.
```

## 4. Hair identity prompt

```text
Create Cassidy's canonical hairstyle reference.

Hair must remain rich dark chocolate brown at its base, with restrained warm espresso and chestnut highlights that appear naturally under light. The hairstyle is layered and highly recognizable in silhouette before individual strands are visible. Use intentional lock groups around the face, controlled side/back volume, and a few signature strands that can become part of Cassidy's identity.

The finish is soft and naturally directional, never metallic or plastic. Avoid excessive strand noise. The hairstyle must work in front, three-quarter, side and rear views and remain readable from a stylized game camera.

Show how the hair behaves during idle, walking, turning, running, talking and a light breeze while preserving the same canonical silhouette.
```

## 5. Base outfit prompt

```text
Design Cassidy's canonical base outfit for a premium stylized 2.5D/3D learning-adventure game.

She is an explorer, learner and intelligent companion, not a mascot and not a school-uniform character. Create an elegant practical layered jacket or overshirt over a clean top, a deep neutral lower garment, comfortable game-ready footwear, subtle seams, fabric layers, cuffs, stitching and functional pockets.

Use restrained emerald accents as Cassidy's GoPAL signature. The outfit should be beautiful through craftsmanship, material quality, silhouette and detail rather than exaggerated anatomy.

Include Cassidy's permanent small luminous leaf/star/compass-inspired companion charm. The charm has a restrained emerald glow and must remain secondary to Cassidy's face.

Show front, three-quarter and rear outfit views while preserving one exact character identity.
```

## 6. Canonical full-body character-sheet prompt

This prompt is the most important reference-generation prompt before 3D production.

```text
Create a professional canonical character turnaround sheet for Cassidy, a premium stylized 2.5D/3D game character.

Use one identical Cassidy model across every panel. No redesign between views.

Required panels:
1. front full-body
2. front neutral pose
3. three-quarter front
4. side profile
5. rear three-quarter
6. rear full-body
7. close face portrait
8. hands and gesture reference
9. shoes and ground-contact reference
10. signature accessory close-up

Include clean neutral presentation, consistent orthographic-style framing, matching proportions, consistent hair silhouette, identical clothing construction, identical accessory placement, and consistent lighting.

Identity anchors: rich dark chocolate-brown layered hair; expressive deep near-black eyes with subtle warm undertone and layered depth; distinctive friendly face; balanced natural proportions; complete articulated hands; complete feet; practical footwear; elegant practical adventure/learning outfit; restrained emerald signature accent; small luminous leaf/star/compass-inspired companion charm.

Add a small color/material swatch strip for skin, hair base, hair highlights, eyes, outfit main material, outfit accent, shoes and accessory emissive material.

This sheet is a production reference. Prioritize consistency, construction clarity and repeatability over dramatic effects.
```

```text
Preserve exactly: her face, eye design, dark chocolate-brown hair, hairstyle silhouette, facial proportions, body proportions, core silhouette, hands, feet and signature accessory shape.

Only adapt clothing materials, small accessory accents and environmental presentation.

World: [EMERALD VALLEY / JAPANESE WORLD / FRENCH WORLD]

For Emerald Valley: warm natural materials, soft green/gold environmental accents, restrained emerald accessory glow.

For Japanese World: refined travel/adventure styling with restrained indigo, vermilion, cream or jade accents. Avoid turning Cassidy into a costume or stereotype.

For French World: elegant travel/learning styling with restrained navy, burgundy, cream or warm-gold accents. Avoid caricature or costume styling.

The result must immediately read as the same Cassidy in a different place.
```

This is a handoff prompt for a 3D artist or 3D-aware generation workflow.

```text
Translate the approved Cassidy character sheet into a production-ready stylized 3D game-character reference.

Do not reinterpret the character. Treat the approved turnaround and expression sheet as the source of truth.

Required construction:
- full head and face
- layered dark-brown hair with authored hair groups
- expressive multi-layer eyes with gaze control
- brows and facial expression controls
- neck and shoulders
- layered clothing
- articulated arms
- clean articulated hands
- articulated legs
- complete feet and stable shoes
- signature luminous companion accessory

Target a premium stylized game aesthetic with clean topology, readable forms, coherent materials and a silhouette that works from a three-quarter game camera.

Prepare the design so it can later support a full-body skeleton, facial blendshapes or equivalent controls, eye/gaze controls, hand gestures, secondary hair motion and accessory motion.

Mobile optimization must be considered without destroying the character's recognizable face, eyes, hair silhouette or hands.
```

## 11. Negative / exclusion prompt

Append the following to tools that support negative prompts or explicit exclusions:

```text
Do not make Cassidy a generic AI avatar, generic anime girl, school mascot, princess, superhero, fashion model, fantasy costume character, chibi mascot, doll, plastic toy, photoreal celebrity, or random NPC.

Do not change her identity between images.
Do not change her hair to blonde, black-blue, red, pink or another identity-breaking color.
Do not replace her dark brown layered hairstyle with a generic hairstyle.
Do not make the eyes flat brown, blue, green or glowing fantasy eyes; preserve deep near-black expressive eyes with subtle warm undertone.
Do not use pasted white eye highlights.
Do not exaggerate anatomy or sexualize the design.
Do not create implausible hands, missing fingers, fused fingers, missing feet or floating shoes.
Do not make clothing overly revealing, impractical or unrelated to an adventure/learning companion.
Do not cover Cassidy in emerald green.
Do not make the accessory larger or brighter than her face.
Do not add random weapons, wings, crowns, armor or unrelated fantasy props.
Do not introduce a second character identity.
Do not alter her core silhouette for a world variant.
Avoid noisy hair strands, glossy plastic skin, metallic hair, dead eyes, mannequin poses, stiff limbs, broken perspective, inconsistent proportions, inconsistent face geometry and over-rendered visual noise.
```

## 12. Consistency lock

When an external tool supports reference images, character references, seed locking, identity locking, LoRA/fine-tuning, or similar mechanisms:

1. Select **one** approved canonical hero image.
2. Select the approved turnaround sheet as the structural reference.
3. Select the approved expression sheet as the facial reference.
4. Reuse the same references for every later concept variation.
5. Do not use a new random character image as the identity reference.
6. Never let a world variant become the new canonical face/hair reference.
7. Record the chosen asset/version outside the generation tool so later 3D work uses the exact approved identity.

The desired workflow is:

`Identity exploration -> canonical selection -> character sheet -> expression sheet -> pose sheet -> world variants -> 3D reference`

Never:

`random generation -> random generation -> redesign -> 3D -> redesign`

## 14. Canonical approval gate

Phase 1 is visually complete only when there is one approved canonical set containing:

- [ ] one hero/full-body concept
- [ ] one face/eye reference
- [ ] one hair reference
- [ ] one base outfit reference
- [ ] one full turnaround sheet
- [ ] one expression sheet
- [ ] one interaction/pose sheet
- [ ] one material/color reference
- [ ] one signature-accessory reference
- [ ] one locked negative/exclusion specification
- [ ] one recorded source/version for each approved external asset

After approval, do **not** redesign Cassidy casually during 3D production. Future changes should be explicit versioned character-design revisions.

---

## 15. Handoff to Phase 2

Phase 2 begins only from the approved Phase 1 visual identity.

The next production target is the **canonical character sheet and production asset preparation**: front/three-quarter/side/rear consistency, expression construction, outfit construction, material references, and 3D modeling/retopology requirements.

The GoPAL-AI codebase remains responsible for Cassidy's state and intelligence. External art assets express that state; they do not replace Cassidy's engines.

Canonical engineering direction:

`Cassidy engines -> character state -> visual resolver -> canonical Cassidy asset -> expression/gaze/animation`

Never create a second Cassidy intelligence, memory, relationship or state system inside the art renderer.
