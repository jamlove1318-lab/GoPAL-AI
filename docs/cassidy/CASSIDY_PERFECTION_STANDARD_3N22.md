# Cassidy — Perfection Standard 3N.22

## Core principle

**We do not make Cassidy good enough. We make Cassidy as close to perfect as the available art, engineering, and review process can achieve.**

“Perfect” here means an intentionally maintained flagship-quality target, not permission to lower a gate when production is inconvenient.

## The quality hierarchy

Every Cassidy release must optimize in this order:

1. identity
2. facial appeal and expression
3. silhouette and proportions
4. topology and deformation
5. hair construction
6. clothing construction
7. materials and lighting response
8. animation and secondary motion
9. mobile performance
10. consistency across every GoPAL-AI world

Technical validity never compensates for weak visual quality.

## Identity must survive every transformation

The following are invariant:

- face identity
- eye character
- dark chocolate-brown layered hair identity
- core body proportions and silhouette
- signature emerald/gold language
- companion charm identity

World-specific variants may change outfit, surface treatment, contextual accessories, and environmental wear, but must remain unmistakably Cassidy.

## Visual quality target

The hero source should look intentionally authored at close inspection and at normal mobile viewing distance.

Reject:

- primitive-looking anatomy
- generic mannequin faces
- flat or unreadable eyes
- helmet/block hair
- disconnected limbs
- floating accessories
- clothing that reads as painted-on geometry
- accidental faceting where smooth forms are intended
- noisy topology that deforms poorly
- generic stock-character appearance
- inconsistent identity between views

## Technical quality target

Prefer:

- clean predominantly quad topology
- purposeful edge flow around shoulders, elbows, knees, hips, mouth, eyes, and eyelids
- stable deformation under the full animation library
- clean normals
- production UVs
- controlled material count
- organized semantic components
- deterministic source manifests
- mobile-aware LODs
- reproducible exports

The numerical intake thresholds are minimum safety checks only. Passing a threshold does not mean the asset is visually excellent.

## Face

The face receives flagship priority because it is the strongest identity carrier.

It must support:

- neutral expression
- happiness
- curiosity
- surprise
- thoughtfulness
- excitement
- concern
- playfulness
- natural eye focus
- believable blinking
- clean eyelid deformation
- expressive mouth deformation

The face must remain appealing in all canonical views, not only the hero camera.

## Hair

Cassidy's hair is a defining feature.

The final hair should have:

- layered dark chocolate-brown masses
- intentional strand/group direction
- controlled silhouette
- believable volume
- clean attachment to the scalp
- secondary motion where appropriate
- mobile-friendly geometry/material strategy

A block-shaped placeholder is never acceptable as final Cassidy hair.

## Outfit

The base outfit should communicate adventure, learning, practicality, and character personality without overpowering the face.

Construction should include believable seams, layering, closures, material boundaries, and deformation behavior where visible.

## Companion charm

The leaf-star-compass charm is a persistent identity object.

It must have:

- deliberate hard-surface or authored form
- readable silhouette
- emerald/gold signature treatment
- controlled luminous response
- stable attachment
- sensible scale in both close and mobile views

## Animation

The character must feel alive before any gameplay interaction begins.

Required library:

`idle`, `walk`, `run`, `turn`, `sit`, `talk`, `gesture`, `point`, `celebrate`, `think`, `react`

Animation quality includes anticipation, follow-through, timing, eye behavior, facial contribution, and secondary motion where appropriate.

## Five-view rule

Cassidy must remain coherent in:

- front
- 3/4 front
- side
- 3/4 back
- back

A beautiful hero camera cannot conceal broken structure.

## Human approval

Automated analysis may reject assets and provide evidence. It may never declare Cassidy visually perfect.

The final decision remains a deliberate human visual review against this standard.

## Reusable-world rule

Cassidy is a permanent character identity, not a collection of unrelated models.

All future GoPAL-AI worlds should reuse the accepted identity source and derive controlled variants from it.

This prevents identity drift and allows improvements to flow back into a single canonical source.
